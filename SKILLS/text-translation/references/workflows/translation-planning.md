# Translation Planning

## Purpose

Define a translation brief and working terminology/policy from the source, intended readers, and the features whose treatment will affect the result.

## Intake

Identify source/target languages and varieties, source version, genre, audience, extent, purpose, required format, existing translations/glossaries, and requested freedom of adaptation.

## Workflow

1. Inspect representative source passages for register, ambiguity, voice, syntax, sound/form, culture-bound references, names, terminology, and quoted material.
2. Record translation priorities and tensions: semantic precision, readability, ambiguity, rhythm/rhyme, characterization, specialist terminology, or parallel alignment.
3. Create a compact terminology and treatment record, retaining alternatives and source locations where no single target term is yet justified.
4. Define segmentation and coverage suitable to the work, plus a plan for comparative reading and any needed subject/language expertise.
5. If a translation was requested, proceed to translation-drafting with stated reversible choices. Planning alone is not a complete translation.

Read [translation-planning-kit.md](../artifacts/translation-planning-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- Do not silently replace a translation request with a summary, localization, or adaptation.
- A glossary decision is contextual; a repeated source word need not take one target word in every context.
- Preserving ambiguity may be more faithful than selecting one interpretation for fluency.
- Missing preferences permit provisional choices; missing source passages remain missing.

## Verification

- Compare policy choices with the source examples and user priorities.
- Check that all supplied units are represented in the coverage record.
- Identify language or subject uncertainties requiring further verification without implying native-speaker review occurred.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Translation Brief`, `Source Features`, `Terminology And Treatment`, `Coverage Plan`, `Open Choices`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Use `text-translator` for complete drafts, `translation-reviewer` for comparative review, and `text-adaptation-editor` for a requested change of audience or medium.

## Boundaries

Do not claim unseen source coverage, certified translation, or expert language review. Translation planning does not authorize publication or rights clearance.
