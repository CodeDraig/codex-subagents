# Translation Drafting

## Purpose

Produce complete translations and requested translation revisions with deliberate handling of meaning, register, ambiguity, and form.

## Intake

Capture source and version, language varieties, audience, genre, purpose, extent, terminology/policy, supplied prior translation, and whether the task permits adaptation.

## Workflow

1. Read each source unit in context before rendering it. Resolve referents, tense/aspect, negation, modality, idiom, and discourse relations; retain unresolved interpretations explicitly.
2. Translate the full requested extent. Preserve speaker relationships, register, recurring language, specialist meanings, and meaningful formal devices according to the brief.
3. For poetry/drama, negotiate sound, lineation, rhythm, performability, and semantic tradeoffs consciously. For technical/professional prose, preserve conditions, units, warnings, obligations, and defined terms.
4. Read the target independently for idiomatic flow, then compare it against the source for omissions, additions, shifted emphasis, and unjustified disambiguation.
5. Apply requested revisions to the actual translation, update terminology decisions, and deliver the complete target text with concise notes on material choices or uncertainty.

Read [translation-drafting-kit.md](../artifacts/translation-drafting-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- A fluent target can still be inaccurate; independent readability and source comparison are separate passes.
- Do not use back-translation alone as proof of fidelity.
- Keep quotation status and attribution; do not fabricate source-language material to explain a choice.
- If source text is unreadable or missing, mark the affected unit instead of inventing it.

## Verification

- Check source-to-target coverage unit by unit, especially numbers, names, negation, modality, and repeated terms.
- Compare register, ambiguity, emphasis, and form with the agreed priorities.
- Report unresolved language/subject issues and distinguish your review from external expert certification.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Translation`, `Translation Decisions`, `Terminology Notes`, `Source Queries`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Hand comparative assessment to `translation-reviewer`, source-witness questions to `textual-witness-analyst`, and publication proofs to `proofreader`.

## Boundaries

Full translation and substantial revision are supported. Do not certify legal/official validity, imply unperformed expert review, or present omitted source units as translated.
