# Translation Review: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Issue record: source location/text | target location/text | category | consequence | correction/options | confidence.
- Severity guide: reversed condition/negation or omitted content; material nuance; terminology inconsistency; optional preference.
- Coverage log: aligned units | sampled/unread units | missing sources | review limits.

## Worked Example

Source says an action is permitted only after inspection; the target says it is required after inspection. Report the permission-to-obligation shift with both locations and a correction. A separate choice between two equally suitable idioms is recorded as optional style, not as an accuracy failure.

## Failure Case

Failure: call a translation “fully verified” after checking its opening, or replace a deliberately ambiguous pronoun with a guessed name. Correction: state sample coverage and preserve uncertainty supported by the source.
