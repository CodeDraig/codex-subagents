# Translation Drafting: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Alignment: source unit/location | target unit/location | omissions/additions | status.
- Material choice: source feature | target treatment | alternative | gain/loss | query.
- Comparison pass: numbers/names; negation/modality; register; ambiguity; recurring devices; complete extent.

## Worked Example

Spanish source: “No prometió volver; dijo que quizá escribiría.” A target such as “She did not promise to return; she said she might write” preserves both negation and possibility, while a gender-neutral rendering or query may be needed if context does not establish the subject. “She promised to write” would change the commitment.

## Failure Case

Failure: add gender, certainty, or explanatory facts because the target sounds smoother. Correction: recover what the source actually determines and preserve or query what it leaves open.
