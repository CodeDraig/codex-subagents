# Translation Planning: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Brief: source/target varieties | purpose | reader | form | fidelity/adaptation choices | output.
- Term record: source term/location | sense/context | target options | decision | rationale.
- Coverage: source unit | target unit | draft/review status | unresolved treatment.

## Worked Example

A poem repeats a word that can mean both a physical bank and a store of memory in context. The brief records the ambiguity and two target strategies, one preserving an ambiguous term and one using a note. Neither is called the unique correct rendering before the user’s priorities and full poem are considered.

## Failure Case

Failure: translate a refrain differently each time without noticing its structural role, or require every uncertain word to be settled before any drafting. Correction: track the refrain, mark local choices, and continue the rest of the translation.
