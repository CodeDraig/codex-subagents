# Lexicon And Corpus Curation Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Entry and sense IDs, original form, search key, and orthography.
- Meaning or analysis, evidence status, and alternative interpretation.
- Example text, utterance/segment ID, source version, and accessible anchor.
- Speaker/session or variety evidence and relevant restriction.
- Proposed merge, split, or variant relation with retained original IDs.
- Unattested field, broken link, or speaker-review question.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

Synthetic entries E1 and E2 both spell tal. U7 supports E1 meaning stone; U9 supports E2 meaning count. The curator preserves two sense records and labels their lexical relationship unresolved. E3 has form taal in another variety; a similar English gloss does not justify merging it into tal. All three retain their own source links.

## Failure Case

A cleanup routine removes diacritics and merges all equal search keys. This destroys contrasts and provenance. Keep original forms, use a separate optional search key, and review candidate merges against attested meaning and variety.
