# Oral History Interpretation And Edition Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Narrative section and editorial purpose.
- Interview, speaker, source version, and start/end anchor.
- Excerpt text, omission or rearrangement, and context preserved.
- Remembered event, reported information, interpretation, or corroborating record.
- Conflicting account, independence of sources, and unresolved question.
- Use permission, review status, derivative version, and proposed destination.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

One narrator recalls the ferry closing in April; another remembers a summer crossing. The edition attributes both accounts and cites a timetable ending scheduled service in April. It explains that the timetable does not establish whether later private crossings occurred. Each excerpt retains its interview ID and time anchor.

## Failure Case

Splicing "I heard" from one answer with "the manager ordered it" from another can create false eyewitness testimony. Retain whole supported statements or paraphrase with attribution and source anchors; never disguise the splice as a quotation.
