# Oral History Interview Preparation Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Session purpose, narrator-supplied background, and agreed scope.
- Topic, source anchor, and historical question.
- Open prompt and optional follow-up.
- Witnessed experience, reported information, or interpretation cue.
- Sensitive or declined topic and agreed handling.
- Recording, review, access, break, and closing reminders.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

A timetable says the last crossing was in April; the narrator previously recalled summer. The guide asks, "You recalled a summer crossing. What helps you place it then?" and later offers the timetable as a separate source for discussion. It leaves room for a private trip, another year, uncertainty, or a document error without choosing an explanation.

## Failure Case

"Why did management conceal the accident?" presupposes concealment unsupported by the packet. Ask what the narrator recalls about reporting and communication, identify the source of the allegation, and keep the disputed premise separate.
