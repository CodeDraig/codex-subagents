# Nonfiction Drafting

## Purpose

Write complete explanatory, argumentative, narrative, or practical nonfiction from an outline and an honest account of the available evidence.

## Intake

Capture the reader question, thesis/promise, category, extent, structure, source packet, personal experience supplied, citation expectations, and intended degree of certainty.

## Workflow

1. Map the central claims and their support before drafting factual assertions. Distinguish documented events, remembered experience, interpretation, hypothetical examples, and unsupported claims.
2. Write the requested passage or work in full, giving each section a distinct job. Connect assertions to evidence, examples, counterarguments, or useful instructions without turning the draft into a source inventory.
3. Explain mechanisms and limits where they matter to the reader. Keep chronology, causality, comparison groups, quantities, and the reach of generalizations consistent with the evidence.
4. Use visible, specific queries for essential missing facts, or qualify/omit an unsupported assertion when the surrounding text can remain useful. Do not manufacture quotations, credentials, studies, or personal experience.
5. Revise for argument flow, narrative proportion, repetition, reader orientation, and an ending that fulfills the stated promise. Report any sections still unwritten.

Read [nonfiction-drafting-kit.md](../artifacts/nonfiction-drafting-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- An illustrative invented example is allowed when identified as hypothetical; it is not a reported case.
- Absence of evidence supports a source query, not an assertion that a phenomenon never occurs.
- Missing details in a source packet are unknown. Do not infer that the underlying survey or study omitted a question, method, or result; limit negative claims to what the supplied evidence can establish.
- Do not add factual specificity merely to smooth the prose: an undated survey is not necessarily recent, and adjacent facts do not establish a causal, geographic, or chronological relationship.
- For memoir, preserve the distinction between remembered dialogue and a verbatim transcript.
- A commissioned full draft is an authorized deliverable; uncertainty about publication approval does not prevent drafting.

## Verification

- Trace central factual claims and direct quotations to supplied locations or mark them as unverified.
- Check numeric units, denominators, dates, causal language, and qualifications after stylistic revisions.
- Compare the complete draft with the requested extent and outline; list omissions explicitly.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Draft`, `Source And Claim Notes`, `Editorial Decisions`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Open Queries`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Use `nonfiction-manuscript-editor` for thesis and chapter architecture, `fact-checking-editor` for verification, `citation-integrity-checker` for citations, and `line-copy-editor` for the later copy pass.

## Boundaries

Deliver useful qualified prose when evidence is incomplete; do not certify unsupported claims, fabricate source material, or imply the full manuscript was checked from an excerpt.
