# Composition Planning

## Purpose

Turn an intention, commission, fragment, or open-ended idea into a usable writing brief and a route from exploration to finished text.

## Intake

Capture the desired work, audience, language/dialect, form, length, tone, creative constraints, available material, deadline, and whether the user wants exploration, a plan, or writing now. Separate fixed choices from preferences and unknowns.

## Workflow

1. Generate meaningfully different concepts when the premise is open: vary the central question, perspective, situation, or organizing device rather than producing synonymous titles.
2. Test candidate concepts against the intended reader experience and available material. Explain the specific tension, argument, image, or use that can sustain each concept.
3. Create the smallest brief that can guide drafting: purpose, audience promise, form, voice, scope, source status, constraints, and a provisional structure. Mark reversible assumptions without turning every choice into an approval gate.
4. Map the work into concrete units and passes: research or invention, outline, drafting, structural revision, line/copy editing, proof, and package. Select only stages the assignment needs.
5. Identify the next executable writing task and its input/output. If writing was requested, continue to the relevant drafting mode; do not finish with planning alone.

Read [composition-planning-kit.md](../artifacts/composition-planning-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- An exploratory poem or fragment need not acquire a thesis, commercial audience, or conventional plot.
- A proposed concept is a working choice, not evidence that the author has approved it. Minor reversible choices can be explicit assumptions.
- For book-length requests, record unit IDs, target extent, source dependencies, completed text, and remaining coverage; an outline is not a completed manuscript.

## Verification

- Compare the brief with every explicit user constraint and resolve contradictions before a dependent irreversible decision.
- Check that each planned unit has a distinct purpose and that the final deliverable is represented, including format or submission needs when requested.
- Report planning completeness separately from text actually drafted.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Writing Brief`, `Concept Options`, `Chosen Working Direction`, `Structure And Coverage`, `Next Writing Task`, `Open Decisions`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Hand full texts to `fiction-drafter`, `nonfiction-drafter`, `essay-writer`, `poetry-writer`, `dramatic-text-writer`, `professional-text-writer`, or `academic-manuscript-writer`. Use `production-editor` for a requested production schedule.

## Boundaries

Do not invent a personal history, evidence packet, commission, or author approval. Missing preferences permit a qualified working brief; missing source facts remain open rather than becoming invented nonfiction.
