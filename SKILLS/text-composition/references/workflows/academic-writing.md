# Academic Writing

## Purpose

Compose complete scholarly manuscript sections or papers from supplied research, reasoning, results, and sources while preserving the evidential record.

## Intake

Capture discipline, article/section type, research question, target audience or venue, source/results packet, method details, citation style, authorship context, and reporting constraints.

## Workflow

1. Map the research question, contribution, prior work, methods, results, and limits that the supplied evidence can support. For conceptual or humanities work, map the argument and primary-text analysis instead of imposing an empirical template.
2. Draft the requested complete sections with a clear relationship between question, evidence, interpretation, and contribution. Separate reported results from discussion and speculation.
3. Integrate citations only from verified supplied sources or authorized research. Use specific citation queries when support is missing; never generate a plausible-looking bibliography entry from memory as if verified.
4. Check whether the abstract, title, introduction, results, discussion, and conclusion agree on sample, scope, outcomes, uncertainty, and novelty.
5. Revise for disciplinary clarity, economical signposting, definitions, reproducibility detail where relevant, and proportionate claims. Preserve contrary evidence and limitations.

Read [academic-writing-kit.md](../artifacts/academic-writing-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- Do not fabricate participants, procedures, ethics approvals, statistical outputs, quotations, or references to complete a conventional section.
- A null or uncertain result is not a writing defect to be edited into success.
- A paper draft can proceed with explicit missing-method or citation queries; it must not present those gaps as resolved.
- Complete drafting and rewriting are supported without falsely claiming human contributions, review acceptance, or research actions.

## Verification

- Trace quantities, method statements, quotations, and central claims to supplied locations.
- Compare abstract and conclusion claims with actual results and limitations; inspect tables/figures referenced in prose if supplied.
- Check required section/word limits and report exactly which materials were inspected.
- When reporting an exact word count, count the delivered prose with an available counter such as `wc -w` and name any excluded title or metadata. Label an unmeasured count as an estimate.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Manuscript Draft`, `Argument And Evidence Map`, `Methods Or Citation Queries`, `Consistency Checks`, `Coverage`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Use `literature-reviewer`, `research-methods-reviewer`, and `citation-integrity-checker` for their evidence checks; hand review preparation to `peer-review-prep-editor` and venue packaging to `journal-submission-specialist`.

## Boundaries

Do not fabricate research or conceal evidence gaps. Drafting and substantive editing are permitted; actual submission and claims of coauthor or institutional approval require their own evidence.
