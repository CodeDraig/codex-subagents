# Essay Composition

## Purpose

Compose complete reflective, personal, critical, lyric, or braided essays whose movement comes from inquiry as well as argument.

## Intake

Identify the essay form, subject/question, audience, extent, narrative persona, supplied memories or observations, source material, and desired openness or closure.

## Workflow

1. Locate the generative question or tension and the concrete material through which it can be explored: scene, object, quotation, observation, contradiction, or recurring image.
2. Choose a movement suitable to the essay: cumulative argument, association, braid, digression-and-return, juxtaposition, or another requested pattern. Do not require a five-paragraph thesis form.
3. Write the complete essay. Move between particular details and reflection; let each return to an image or question change its meaning rather than simply repeat it.
4. Check the narrator and source contract. Use only supplied autobiographical facts as the user’s experience; clearly frame an invented persona or fictional essay when that is the assignment.
5. Revise transitions, proportion, emotional/intellectual turns, and the ending’s relationship to the opening. Retain productive uncertainty rather than appending a generic moral.

Read [essay-composition-kit.md](../artifacts/essay-composition-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- A personal essay need not resolve its central question, but the ending should register a development in perception.
- Associative structure needs legible relationships; fragmentation alone does not establish a braid.
- Deliberate ambiguity is different from fabricated autobiography or a falsely attributed quotation.

## Verification

- Read for what changes across sections and identify any repetition that adds no pressure or perspective.
- Check supplied memories, quotations, and named facts against the packet.
- Confirm that voice, form, length, and the degree of closure match the brief.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Essay`, `Form And Voice Notes`, `Source Or Memory Queries`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Use `rhetorical-style-editor` for cadence and figurative choices, `nonfiction-manuscript-editor` for sustained argument, and `fact-checking-editor` for factual material.

## Boundaries

Original essays and substantial rewrites are supported. Do not claim invented lived experience belongs to the user or silently replace an exploratory essay with a conventional argument.
