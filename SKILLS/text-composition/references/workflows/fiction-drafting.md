# Fiction Drafting

## Purpose

Write complete original fiction or requested scene/chapter revisions from a brief, including experimental and nonlinear forms.

## Intake

Identify requested extent, genre or artistic frame, narrator/POV, tense, characters, setting/canon, opening and ending constraints, prohibited changes, and whether the task is a new draft or a rewrite.

## Workflow

1. Choose the dramatic or formal engine: desire under pressure, an evolving relationship, a pattern of images, withheld information, or another mechanism appropriate to the work.
2. Draft the requested text in full. Make scenes change the situation through action, perception, choice, or revelation; use summary deliberately rather than replacing every scene with explanation.
3. Control viewpoint access, temporal order, dialogue attribution, physical action, and information release. Carry recurring images or formal devices through meaningful variation.
4. Revise the draft against the brief: motivation, escalation or designed stasis, pacing, continuity, tonal register, and ending effect. Preserve intended ambiguity.
5. For longer work, maintain a unit ledger and canon notes, continue through the requested units, and identify any actual remaining text. Deliver prose, not a substitute synopsis.

Read [fiction-drafting-kit.md](../artifacts/fiction-drafting-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- Genre expectations are a lens chosen by the user, not a requirement to normalize experimental fiction.
- Invent fictional events freely within the brief; do not pass invented nonfiction sources or real quotations off as evidence.
- A scene can turn through changed understanding rather than overt conflict; do not add confrontation mechanically.
- When revising supplied fiction, preserve intentional repetitions, dialect, and structural oddities unless changing them is assigned.

## Verification

- Read the draft as a reader for orientation, emotional/logical consequences, and ending payoff.
- Check requested length/form, POV/tense, named constraints, and recurring entity/timeline facts; count words or units when a hard limit matters.
- Distinguish deliberate ambiguity from accidental missing antecedents or inconsistent facts.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Draft`, `Craft Decisions`, `Continuity Notes`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Open Questions`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Hand structural diagnosis to `fiction-development-editor`, sentence work to `line-copy-editor` or `rhetorical-style-editor`, and cross-manuscript continuity to `manuscript-continuity-reviewer`.

## Boundaries

Full original and commissioned drafting is supported. Do not claim someone approved or authored text when that is untrue, copy protected source passages beyond the permitted task, or misrepresent incomplete coverage as a finished work.
