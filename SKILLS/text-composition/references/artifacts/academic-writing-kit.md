# Academic Writing: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Section contract: purpose | evidence supplied | claims allowed | missing items | target extent.
- Results-to-claims map: result/location | uncertainty | interpretation | forbidden overstatement.
- Cross-section check: sample; timeframe; method; outcome; limitations; contribution.

## Worked Example

Packet: an exploratory study of 24 volunteers found a 3-point mean difference, with no uncertainty interval supplied. The abstract reports the sample and observed difference, labels the analysis exploratory, and leaves a query for uncertainty information. It does not infer statistical significance or a population-wide effect.

## Failure Case

Failure: invent p < .05 or an ethics approval number to make the manuscript look complete. Correction: preserve the supplied result, place a specific query, and keep the substantive draft moving without falsifying the research record.
