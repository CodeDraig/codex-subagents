# Nonfiction Drafting: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Claim ledger: location | assertion | source/location | status | wording constraint.
- Section map: reader question | answer/turn | example/evidence | transition.
- Draft checks: complete extent; attributed quotations; uncertainty retained; examples labeled; no invented biography.

## Worked Example

Packet: 18 of 30 respondents used the library weekly; the survey reached one neighborhood. Draft: “In this neighborhood survey, 18 of 30 respondents reported weekly library use.” The next paragraph can explain this local pattern, but cannot claim a citywide majority. A hypothetical reader scenario is introduced as an illustration, not an interview.

## Failure Case

Failure: improve “was associated with” to “caused” for force, or invent a satisfied patron quotation. Correction: retain the observed relationship and either use the supplied testimony or write a plainly hypothetical example.
