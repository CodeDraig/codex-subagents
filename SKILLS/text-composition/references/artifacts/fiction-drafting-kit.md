# Fiction Drafting: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Draft brief: extent | POV/tense | central pressure | form | fixed canon | ending constraint.
- Scene/unit ledger: ID | situation before | action/perception | change | facts introduced | status.
- Revision check: reader orientation; causality; image/form development; dialogue ownership; ending consequence.

## Worked Example

Brief: a complete 250-word scene, close third person, no revelation of the sealed letter. A draft begins: “Mara held the kettle above the cup until the whistle stopped. Across the table, her brother turned the envelope face down.” The scene develops their negotiation over who will keep it; its ending changes custody without opening it. The delivered artifact contains the whole scene and preserves the letter constraint.

## Failure Case

Failure: return a scene outline or open the letter to manufacture a twist. Correction: write the complete scene and obtain its turn from the permitted relationship or action.
