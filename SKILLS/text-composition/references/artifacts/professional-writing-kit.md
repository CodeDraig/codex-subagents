# Professional Writing: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Communication brief: reader | relationship | decision/action | known facts | commitments allowed | channel.
- Claim/commitment audit: wording | supporting material | authority supplied | open issue.
- Recipient check: answer/request; reason; evidence; responsibility; next step.

## Worked Example

Brief: a memo proposing a two-week pilot, with no approved budget. The draft says “I propose a two-week pilot” and asks the operations owner to confirm staffing; it does not announce a funded launch. Its opening gives the decision needed, followed by the observed problem and the proposed evaluation.

## Failure Case

Failure: turn a proposed pilot into “We will launch Monday” or invent savings. Correction: retain proposal status, state the actual evidence, and leave budget or timing decisions with the named owner.
