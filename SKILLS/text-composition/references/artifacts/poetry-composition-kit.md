# Poetry Composition: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Poem brief: speaker/addressee | pressure | form | length | images | protected wording.
- Line/stanza audit: ending word | syntactic carry | sound relation | turn or emphasis.
- Fixed-form record: requested rule | observed feature | deliberate departure | uncertainty.

## Worked Example

Brief: six lines, repeat “still” at each opening, no rhyme requirement. A draft begins “still the stair keeps / the weight of your leaving”. The repeated opening is preserved through all six lines; line breaks change how “keeps” and later verbs unfold. Review reports six lines and retained anaphora without claiming a metrical form.

## Failure Case

Failure: replace every “still” with a synonym for variety or label the poem iambic without checking stress. Correction: treat the refrain as intentional structure and report only the form actually present.
