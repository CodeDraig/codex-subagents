# Composition Planning: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Brief: desired artifact, reader, language, form, length, voice, fixed choices, optional preferences.
- Concept comparison: central engine; material available; reader effect; cost or uncertainty.
- Unit ledger: ID | purpose | inputs | target extent | draft status | next pass.
- Completion definition: what files or text will exist, and which checks can be performed.

## Worked Example

Request: a 900-word speculative story about a town that rents memories, told through receipts. Candidate A follows a disputed refund; B accumulates receipts across generations. A better fits the short extent because one disputed transaction can carry the conflict. Working brief: six receipts with changing annotations, one clerk viewpoint, no explanatory prologue. Ledger lists R1–R6 as unwritten; the next task is the complete six-part draft.

## Failure Case

Failure: replace the receipt form with a conventional three-act outline and announce that the story is complete. Correction: preserve the form, identify its narrative mechanism, and distinguish the plan from actual drafted receipts.
