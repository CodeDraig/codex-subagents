# Dramatic Writing: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Scene record: location/time | present characters | objectives | tactics | turn | exit state.
- Cue check: speaker/action | addressee | prop/position | required sound or silence.
- Production constraints: cast ceiling | space | effects | timing evidence | unknowns.

## Worked Example

Brief: two actors, one bench, no lighting changes. One wants the other to admit they missed a train; the other keeps offering the bench seat. The scene’s tactics shift through offers and refusals, and a final exchanged ticket changes the relationship. The script supplies the whole scene and uses only the bench and a permitted handheld ticket.

## Failure Case

Failure: solve the scene with a third character entering or call a two-page draft “exactly four minutes” without a read-through. Correction: find the turn within the two-person action and label timing as estimated.
