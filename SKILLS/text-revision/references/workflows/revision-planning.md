# Revision Planning

## Purpose

Turn an existing draft and revision goals into a sequenced, located plan that distinguishes structural work from later sentence polish.

## Intake

Capture manuscript/version, inspected extent, user goals, protected elements, feedback, audience/form, requested edit depth, and whether the assignment includes applying revisions.

## Workflow

1. Read the supplied draft at its meaningful structural scale and map units, promises, claims, or turns. Record what is absent from the supplied material.
2. Identify issues through located examples and reader consequences. Separate author preferences, inconsistencies, missing support, and speculative interpretations.
3. Rank changes by dependency and effect: central architecture before dependent transitions, continuity before factual/style consistency, and final proof after text stabilizes.
4. Write concrete revision actions with affected locations, intended result, preserved elements, dependencies, and a way to evaluate the change.
5. If applied revision is requested, continue through structural-revision or the relevant specialist. A plan alone does not satisfy an editing assignment.

Read [revision-planning-kit.md](../artifacts/revision-planning-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- Do not treat every departure from a conventional arc or essay structure as a defect.
- Separate evidence of a problem from the proposed solution; more than one revision may solve the same issue.
- A partial manuscript permits a scoped plan, not conclusions about unseen chapters.
- Priorities follow the user’s purpose rather than a universal publishing sequence.

## Verification

- Trace each high-priority action to a draft location or explicit user goal.
- Check that actions preserve fixed constraints and do not contradict one another.
- Verify that deferred work is explicitly listed and that any claimed applied change exists.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Revision Scope`, `Draft Map`, `Located Findings`, `Revision Sequence`, `Preserved Choices`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Use `developmental-manuscript-editor` for structural execution, `fiction-development-editor` or `nonfiction-manuscript-editor` for form-specific diagnosis, and `line-copy-editor` after structural dependencies settle.

## Boundaries

Do not infer permission to overwrite a different version or discard author decisions. Missing context permits a bounded plan with explicit limits.
