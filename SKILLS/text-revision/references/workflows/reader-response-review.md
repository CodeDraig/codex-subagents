# Reader Response Review

## Purpose

Evaluate likely reader orientation, comprehension, engagement, and access barriers through located evidence while separating predictions from observed reader feedback.

## Intake

Capture intended readers, reading purpose, form, supplied draft/version, accessibility goals, actual feedback if any, and the desired review scope.

## Workflow

1. Follow the text in reading order and record where a reader must infer an antecedent, term, time shift, motive, argument link, or instruction.
2. Assess whether difficulty serves the intended experience or obstructs it. Preserve deliberate ambiguity, estrangement, dense form, and demanding subject matter when they are the brief.
3. Inspect concrete access concerns: undefined terms, unclear headings, unexplained diagrams, assumed prior knowledge, overloaded instructions, or inaccessible text-dependent cues.
4. Separate observations about the text, predictions about readers, and evidence from actual readers. Do not impersonate demographic experience or claim that a readability score measures understanding.
5. Return prioritized, located recommendations with the intended reader effect and a way to test the change.

Read [reader-response-review-kit.md](../artifacts/reader-response-review-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- A lower reading level is not a universal goal; the intended audience and artistic purpose govern.
- Subjective disengagement is a reviewer response, not proof that all readers will disengage.
- A sensitivity or access concern should name the textual mechanism and uncertainty, not claim representative lived experience.
- Actual feedback must retain source, context, and limits; do not invent a user study.

## Verification

- Anchor each major concern to a passage and the stated audience/purpose.
- Label predicted versus observed effects throughout the report.
- When using counts or readability tools, report their narrow measurement and inspect the passage directly.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Reader Context`, `Located Observations`, `Predicted Reader Effects`, `Observed Feedback`, `Revision Options`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Send applied revisions to `developmental-manuscript-editor`, plain-language adaptations to `text-adaptation-editor`, and exported-document accessibility checks to `publication-format-reviewer`.

## Boundaries

Do not certify accessibility, sensitivity, comprehension, or market response from a textual review alone. Preserve original files and distinguish inference from reader evidence.
