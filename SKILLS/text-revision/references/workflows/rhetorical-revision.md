# Rhetorical Revision

## Purpose

Revise prose for cadence, emphasis, figurative force, register, and syntactic movement while preserving meaning and deliberate voice.

## Intake

Capture the passage, purpose, reader, register/dialect, desired effect, edit intensity, protected language, and whether to apply changes or provide alternatives.

## Workflow

1. Identify the intended movement of attention or feeling and locate the language currently carrying it: clause order, repeated positions, sentence lengths, images, sounds, pauses, or withheld information.
2. Choose changes for their effect rather than adding figures decoratively. Test parallelism, reversal, repetition, ellipsis, question, conjunction, or syntactic inversion only where they serve the passage.
3. Revise the actual passage at the requested intensity, preserving factual qualifications, speaker identity, intentional register shifts, and productive ambiguity.
4. Compare the revised cadence and emphasis with the original. Explain a small number of consequential choices instead of annotating every word.
5. Check whether stronger phrasing has changed the claim, referent, emotional stance, or relationship to the reader.

Read [rhetorical-revision-kit.md](../artifacts/rhetorical-revision-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- Deliberate repetition, fragments, dialect, and long sentences are not errors merely because they depart from plain prose.
- Emphasis cannot strengthen a factual assertion beyond its evidence.
- A device that is conspicuous but weakens the intended effect should be removed.
- Offer distinct alternatives only when they represent real choices of effect; do not replace the requested revision with a catalog of devices.

## Verification

- Read or subvocalize the revised passage for rhythm and unintended emphasis.
- Compare factual scope, negation, modality, referents, and voice before/after.
- Verify protected phrases and requested length or register constraints.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Revised Text`, `Rhetorical Decisions`, `Meaning And Voice Checks`, `Alternatives Or Queries`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Hand grammar/house-style consistency to `line-copy-editor`, larger structure to `developmental-manuscript-editor`, and verse-form decisions to `poetry-writer`.

## Boundaries

Do not hide substantive meaning changes inside a style pass. Full rewrites are allowed when assigned, but protected facts and creative constraints remain binding.
