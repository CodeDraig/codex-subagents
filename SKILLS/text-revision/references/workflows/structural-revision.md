# Structural Revision

## Purpose

Apply substantial manuscript revisions to argument, scene sequence, chapter architecture, transitions, or emphasis while retaining the author’s required choices.

## Intake

Identify the authoritative draft/version, revision brief or findings, owned units, protected wording/form, available evidence, requested extent, and desired delivery format.

## Workflow

1. Map current units to the intended structure. Explain relocations, cuts, expansions, combinations, and new connective material before changing dependent references.
2. Revise the actual text across the assigned scope, including transitions and setup/payoff or claim/evidence relationships affected by movement.
3. Reconcile names, terms, timeline, section labels, cross-references, and repeated explanations after structural changes. Preserve the distinction between fictional invention and source-dependent nonfiction.
4. Keep a before/after unit map and a concise change rationale. Retain recoverable originals and reconcile concurrent changes rather than replacing them blindly.
5. Read the revised text continuously for flow and the original problem. Deliver the actual revised artifact and identify any still-unwritten or uninspected units.

Read [structural-revision-kit.md](../artifacts/structural-revision-kit.md) when preparing the working record or checking the example against a similar request.

## Decision Rules

- A structural edit can require new prose, not just rearrangement. Full rewrites are supported when requested.
- Preserve the user’s experimental arrangement unless its alteration is assigned; solve orientation problems within that frame where possible.
- Do not remove inconvenient counterevidence to strengthen an argument.
- A moved paragraph may invalidate a pronoun, heading reference, chronology, or later conclusion; check the affected chain.

## Verification

- Compare revised units with the brief and original to confirm protected facts and choices survive.
- Check affected transitions, references, sequence dependencies, and claims after movement.
- Use a diff or unit comparison when editing files; inspect the complete assigned span, not only changed lines.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Revised Text`, `Structural Changes`, `Unit Map`, `Preserved Choices`, `Open Queries`, `Coverage And Checks`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include supplied source or manuscript locations where relevant; distinguish applied changes, recommendations, unverified details, and coverage still outstanding. Put the requested text in the text section or identify the actual output file.

## Handoffs

Hand language refinement to `rhetorical-style-editor` or `line-copy-editor`, continuity to `manuscript-continuity-reviewer`, and source questions to `fact-checking-editor`.

## Boundaries

Do not overwrite unowned drafts or silently erase substantive material. Missing evidence stays visible; completed revisions must be distinguishable from proposed actions.
