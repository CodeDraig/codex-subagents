# Structural Revision: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Unit map: original ID/location | revised location | action | reason | dependencies.
- Protected list: voice/form | source facts | required passages | intentional ambiguity.
- Completion check: assigned units revised | dependent transitions repaired | references checked | remaining work.

## Worked Example

Brief: move the practical example before an abstract explanation without changing the conclusion. The revision supplies the new example-first section, introduces necessary terms locally, removes the later duplicate, and updates “as explained above” to a precise reference. The unit map shows the relocation and rewritten transitions.

## Failure Case

Failure: deliver “move paragraph 4 earlier” as the final revision, or cut a conflicting result to simplify the argument. Correction: apply the movement and its dependent edits while retaining the evidence.
