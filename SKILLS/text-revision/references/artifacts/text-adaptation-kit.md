# Text Adaptation: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Transformation brief: source/version | target reader/form | length | protected content | invention allowed.
- Unit map: source location | target location | transformation | information lost/added | reason.
- Fidelity check: claim strength; negation; chronology; source voice; necessary caveats.

## Worked Example

Source: a 1,500-word report with a limited local survey. Target: a 250-word community notice. Retain the local scope and respondent count, remove technical detail with a link/reference to the report, and explain the practical implication without calling the sample representative of the whole city.

## Failure Case

Failure: shorten “may help under these conditions” to “works” or manufacture a local resident’s story. Correction: preserve the qualification and label any permitted illustrative example.
