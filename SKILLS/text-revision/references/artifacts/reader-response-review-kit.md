# Reader Response Review: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Reader friction log: location | task/expectation | inference required | intended difficulty? | proposed change.
- Evidence label: textual observation | reviewer interpretation | actual reader evidence.
- Evaluation option: revised passage | question to test | audience needed | evidence not yet available.

## Worked Example

A guide first uses “snapshot” in a deletion warning before defining it two pages later. The review identifies a likely comprehension barrier for first-time users and recommends an inline definition. It does not claim that users failed a test; no reader sessions were supplied.

## Failure Case

Failure: rewrite a deliberately disorienting story into simple chronology because it scores as difficult. Correction: assess whether the intended disorientation remains navigable and propose changes within the chosen form.
