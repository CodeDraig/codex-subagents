# Variant Collation: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Locus row: anchor | witness A reading/location | witness B reading/location | difference class | coverage | uncertainty.
- Policy record: retained/suppressed differences | normalization mapping | segmentation | base/parallel method.
- Alignment check: repeated phrase; transposition; omitted segment; lacuna; boundary mismatch.

## Worked Example

A reads “the red gate”; B reads “the old red gate”; C is damaged at “red”. Record B’s added “old” at the aligned locus and C’s unreadable span separately. If punctuation is ignored for this pass, retain the raw punctuation in the witness transcription rather than erasing it.

## Failure Case

Failure: list C as supporting omission of “red” or infer that B copied A from one shared phrase. Correction: distinguish coverage from readings and leave genealogical interpretation outside the collation evidence.
