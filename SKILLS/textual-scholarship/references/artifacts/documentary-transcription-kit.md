# Documentary Transcription: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Policy: breaks | spelling/punctuation | abbreviation expansion | additions/deletions | gaps | uncertainty | normalization.
- Unit record: witness/location | transcription | uncertain characters | intervention | verification status.
- Intervention log: source reading | normalized/supplied reading | reason | responsible editor | location.

## Worked Example

Source line reads “ye olde gate” with the next word illegible. Under a diplomatic policy, transcribe “ye olde gate [illegible: 1 word]” and declare the bracket convention. A requested reading version may modernize “olde” to “old”, but the source transcription and the normalization entry remain available.

## Failure Case

Failure: silently output “the old gate opened” because that sounds likely. Correction: preserve the actual readable letters, mark the gap, and keep modernization or conjecture separately identified.
