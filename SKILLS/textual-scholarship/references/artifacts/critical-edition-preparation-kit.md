# Critical Edition Preparation: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Edition policy: purpose | witnesses | base/parallel choice | selection principles | normalization | apparatus scope.
- Intervention record: locus | witness readings | adopted reading | reason | attested/conjectural status | uncertainty.
- Consistency audit: text ↔ apparatus anchors; sigla ↔ witness list; notes ↔ readings; policy ↔ actual treatment.

## Worked Example

A reads “cold light”, B “clear light”; the editor retains A under a documentary policy and records B in the apparatus. An explanatory note can discuss both effects without calling B wrong. A proposed “pale light” is labeled conjectural and is not assigned to either witness.

## Failure Case

Failure: normalize both witnesses to the editor’s preferred wording and then claim unanimous support. Correction: restore the distinct attestations, explain the editorial selection, and retain the intervention record.
