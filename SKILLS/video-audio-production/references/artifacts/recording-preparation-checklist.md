# Recording Preparation Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Capture ID and script segment.
- Location, setup, participant.
- Framing or audio purpose.
- Equipment and storage need.
- Setup and recording minutes.
- Sync or slate identifier.
- Consent and location evidence status.
- Accessibility or safety owner.
- Pickup or backup option.
- Handoff file-label rule.

## Review Checks

- Include room tone or ambient capture where the edit depends on it.
- Keep continuity-sensitive props and wardrobe tied to scene identifiers.
- Unknown consent remains pending even if the participant appears on an old recording.
