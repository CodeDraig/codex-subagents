# Media Delivery Review Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Deliverable ID, path, and version.
- Destination spec and version.
- Requirement and measured or inspected value.
- Evidence tool or source.
- Inspection range and coverage.
- Caption language and cue coverage.
- Content-reference version.
- Pass, fail, or unverified status.
- Correction owner.
- Approval evidence and remaining gate.

## Review Checks

- Check caption end times against the actual export duration.
- A successful video probe may leave audio content and captions unchecked.
- Compare each language and aspect-ratio variant separately; do not inherit a pass from another export.
