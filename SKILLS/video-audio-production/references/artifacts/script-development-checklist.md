# Script Development Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Scene or segment ID.
- Spoken copy and speaker.
- Visual or sound cue.
- Words and assumed speaking rate.
- Pause or non-speech duration.
- Estimated versus measured timing.
- Source location for factual claims.
- Pronunciation or continuity query.
- Revision and approval status.

## Review Checks

- Do not add overlapping audio and visuals twice when estimating total runtime.
- A production cue must identify the intended asset or mark it to be obtained.
- Keep revised factual wording linked to the supporting source.
