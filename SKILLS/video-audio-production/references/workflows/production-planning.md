# Production Planning

## Purpose

Plans video and audio production briefs, deliverables, schedules, dependencies, and resource assumptions before scripting or recording.

## Intake

Restate audience, communication goal, distribution context, format, target runtime, supplied delivery specs, resources, budget assumptions, deadline, and approval owner. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Translate the communication goal into the intended audience response and identify required versus optional deliverables.
2. Record runtime, language, aspect ratio or audio format, captions, transcripts, versions, and destination specifications only when supplied or verified.
3. Work backward from delivery through review, revision, edit, recording, script approval, asset collection, and permissions dependencies.
4. Estimate resource and time needs as assumptions; distinguish confirmed availability from requests not yet made.
5. Return a feasible brief with unresolved decisions, contingency options, and clearly scoped handoffs.

Read [production-planning-checklist.md](../artifacts/production-planning-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- A deadline does not imply crew availability, talent consent, or booked facilities.
- Include review and revision time rather than allocating the entire schedule to recording and editing.
- Treat platform specifications as versioned inputs; do not invent current technical limits.

## Validation

Reconcile deliverables with the brief and work backward through dependency dates. Check runtime and version counts against script and edit scope. Cite supplied specs and mark availability, cost, or technical fields unconfirmed when evidence is missing.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Production Brief`, `Audience And Outcome`, `Deliverables`, `Schedule`, `Resources And Dependencies`, `Risks`, `Approval Points`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand scripts to `script-development-editor`, session preparation to `recording-preparation-coordinator`, edit coordination to `postproduction-coordinator`, delivery checks to `media-delivery-reviewer`, and rights evidence to `permissions-reviewer`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before booking resources, spending money, contacting participants, approving rights, or publishing. Unknown specifications or availability permit a provisional plan with named confirmation points.
