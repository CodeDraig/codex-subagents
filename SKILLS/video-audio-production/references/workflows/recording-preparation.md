# Recording Preparation

## Purpose

Prepares shot and recording lists, session logistics, equipment checks, asset needs, and readiness notes without booking resources or directing unsafe production.

## Intake

Restate approved or draft script, production brief, participants, locations, session duration, supplied equipment, accessibility needs, consent status, and production owner. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Translate each scripted scene or segment into required shots, interviews, narration, room tone, pickups, or supplementary assets.
2. Group capture tasks by location, participant, setup, and continuity needs while preserving script dependencies.
3. Prepare a session schedule including setup, technical checks, breaks, resets, accessibility needs, and backup options.
4. List equipment and media requirements, power, storage, monitoring, file labels, sync cues, and a non-destructive handoff procedure.
5. Check consent, location permission, resource confirmation, safety questions, and unresolved script changes before declaring the session prepared.

Read [recording-preparation-checklist.md](../artifacts/recording-preparation-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- A draft call sheet is not a participant notification or confirmed booking.
- Never format recording media or erase cards as a preparation shortcut.
- Do not improvise hazardous rigging, stunts, electrical work, or location access; identify the qualified production owner.

## Validation

Cross-reference every required scene to a capture task, check the schedule fits the session window, and separate equipment available from equipment assumed. Mark audio, lighting, power, storage, and sync tests as proposed unless results are supplied or checks are authorized and executed.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Session Scope`, `Shot And Recording List`, `Session Schedule`, `Equipment And Assets`, `Participant And Access Needs`, `Readiness Gaps`, `Contingencies`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand script changes to `script-development-editor`, scope or resources to `production-brief-planner`, rights and releases to `permissions-reviewer`, and captured-media handoff planning to `postproduction-coordinator`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before contacting participants, booking locations, spending money, recording people without authority, deleting media, or approving safety arrangements. Continue a draft schedule and readiness-gap list for missing confirmations.
