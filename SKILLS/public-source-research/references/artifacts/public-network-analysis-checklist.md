# Public Network Analysis Checklist

## Scope And Provenance

- Research question, decision, public-interest purpose, and intended audience.
- Platforms, public accounts, URLs, hashtags, languages, time window, and timezone.
- Collection method, query, API or manual fields, sample size, denominator, exclusions, and access date.
- Deleted, private, unavailable, rate-limited, or algorithmically selected content.
- Personal data that is necessary, redacted, aggregated, or excluded.

## Observable Network

- Nodes: public accounts, posts, domains, URLs, hashtags, or content artifacts.
- Edges: repost, reply, mention, shared link, phrase reuse, temporal sequence, or cross-platform reuse.
- Roles: originator, early amplifier, bridge, hub, repeated relay, or isolated participant; describe behavior without asserting intent.
- Record the exact observation supporting each node, edge, or role.

## Coordination Signal Grades

| Strength | Examples | Required caution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weak | Shared hashtag, popular URL, broad viewpoint, common posting hour, or reaction to the same event. | Do not call coordination; establish a baseline. |
| Moderate | Repeated unusual phrase, recurring ordered amplification, narrow timing across several events, or repeated cross-platform reuse. | Test common source, scheduling, fandom, and platform mechanics. |
| Strong | Multiple independent signal classes recur across events, remain unusual against a baseline, and survive duplicate or hub removal. | Supports coordinated behavior, not identity, sponsor, intent, or deception by itself. |
| Inconclusive | Denominator, provenance, timeframe, platform coverage, or alternatives cannot be tested. | State the missing evidence and avoid attribution. |

## Alternative Explanations

- Breaking-news convergence or a shared official announcement.
- Fandom, activism, shared ideology, or a public campaign schedule.
- Platform recommendations, trending surfaces, or common automation tools.
- Syndicated content, copied press material, or one upstream source.
- Timezone, language, sampling, deletion, or API-selection artifacts.

## Sensitivity And Reproducibility

- Repeat the analysis after removing high-degree hubs, exact duplicates, reposts, and known scheduled accounts.
- Compare observed cadence or similarity with an appropriate baseline group and time window.
- For repeated order or synchrony claims, state the statistic, baseline or permutation design, tie handling, event-selection rule, and multiple-comparison treatment before calculating significance.
- Record whether the conclusion changes under alternative edge definitions or thresholds.
- Preserve public identifiers or screenshots only when needed to reproduce a material finding.

## Privacy Gate

- Prefer aggregate counts, clusters, and representative examples over identity lists.
- Do not deanonymize pseudonymous users or infer protected traits, home location, employer, or private associations.
- Do not produce target lists, exposure recommendations, employment consequences, or physical-world actions.
- Escalate publication, enforcement, or attribution decisions to authorized owners.
