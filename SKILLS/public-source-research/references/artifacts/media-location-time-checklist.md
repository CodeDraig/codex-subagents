# Media Location And Time Checklist

## Artifact Ledger

- Record the artifact identifier, source URL or path, uploader or archive, access date, public or user-provided status, claimed location, claimed time, and intended use.
- Preserve the original file when available. Record hashes for the original and working copy if authenticity or edit history is material.
- Record relevant frame numbers or timestamps rather than describing the entire artifact loosely.

## Integrity Checks

- Compare the earliest accessible copy with reposts or derivatives.
- Check crop, mirror, rotation, re-encoding, overlays, splices, missing audio, and inconsistent frame order.
- If mirroring or rotation is plausible, evaluate each orientation as a separate hypothesis and recompute every dependent clock, sign, sun, and shadow inference within that branch.
- Treat absent metadata as unknown, not suspicious by itself.
- Compare metadata with visible lighting, weather, device behavior, upload chronology, and known edits.

## Location Clues

- High-specificity: unique landmark geometry, official sign plus route number, distinctive transit stop, matched skyline, or authoritative geospatial feature.
- Medium-specificity: road markings, language, utility design, terrain, vegetation, architecture, transit livery, or regional signage conventions.
- Low-specificity: clothing, generic vehicles, broad climate, unsourced captions, aesthetic resemblance, or a single common storefront.
- Record whether each clue is directly visible, inferred, sourced externally, or contradicted.

## Time Clues

- Check verified event schedules, construction state, seasonal vegetation, weather history, service changes, public clocks, publication chronology, and dated signage.
- For shadows, record object geometry, apparent sun direction, camera orientation, terrain, assumed date range, and resulting time window.
- Do not count clock and shadow agreement as two independent clues until the artifact orientation they share has been resolved.
- Distinguish capture time, upload time, publication time, and the time represented by reused or archival media.

## Confidence Gate

| Confidence | Minimum evidence shape |
| --- | --- |
| High | Two or more independent, specific clue families agree; integrity concerns and plausible alternatives are resolved. |
| Moderate | One strong clue with independent support, or several medium clues agree; material ambiguity remains. |
| Low | Clues are generic, derivative, weakly sourced, or compatible with several alternatives. |
| Indeterminate | Artifact quality, provenance, source access, or contradictions prevent a defensible assessment. |

Do not convert these labels into exact probabilities without a calibrated method.

## Safety Gate

- State the least precision needed: country, region, city, venue, broad time window, or exact point.
- Remove residential addresses, live positions, routes, schedules, faces, plates, and unrelated identifiers unless already public, necessary, and clearly benign.
- Stop on private-person tracking, doxxing, trespass enablement, or sensitive-facility targeting.
