# Public Records Search Checklist

## Search Scope

- Subject name, aliases, transliterations, former names, and authoritative identifiers.
- Person or entity type, jurisdiction, record type, date range, and purpose.
- Sources included, sources excluded, access limits, query variants, and retrieval date.
- Decision supported and the level of identity confidence required.

## Official Source Plan

- Corporate registry and filing authority.
- Court docket or tribunal system.
- Regulator, licensing body, or enforcement database.
- Procurement, grants, lobbying, or public-spending portal.
- Sanctions or debarment authority with list version and effective date.
- Official archive for changed or removed government pages.

Commercial aggregators and search snippets are discovery aids, not substitutes for the issuing source.

## Entity Disambiguation

| Match level | Evidence shape | Reporting rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Unique authoritative identifier, or three independent attributes with no material contradiction. | Report as a match with the supporting identifiers and source. |
| Probable | Two independent attributes agree and no material contradiction appears, but no unique identifier is available. | Report as probable and state what would confirm it. |
| Possible | Name plus one weak or non-unique attribute. | Keep separate; do not merge records or report adverse facts as belonging to the subject. |
| Excluded | A stable identifier or material attribute conflicts. | Record the non-match and exclusion reason. |

Useful attributes include entity number, case number, jurisdiction, incorporation or birth date, address at the relevant time, officers, counsel, employer, and filing history. Apply privacy minimization even when attributes are public.

If the requested output is binary, do not force `Possible` into match or non-match. Report it as an unresolved candidate under entity disambiguation and state the official evidence needed to resolve it.

## Procedural Context

- Complaint or allegation: a claim was filed or stated; no finding is implied.
- Charge or indictment: an authority initiated a proceeding; guilt is not established.
- Judgment or order: report the issuing body, date, scope, appeal or stay status, and current effect.
- Dismissal, acquittal, settlement, or closure: preserve the exact disposition and do not imply a finding beyond it.
- Registration status: active, inactive, dissolved, expired, suspended, or superseded is an administrative status, not proof of misconduct.
- Sanctions or debarment: record the issuing authority, program, listed party, effective date, current status, and identifier match.

## Privacy And Publication Gate

- Remove home addresses, family details, government identifiers, and unrelated sensitive data.
- Do not expose sealed, expunged, restricted, hacked, or credentialed material.
- Require legal or standards review before publishing serious adverse records about an identifiable person.
- State the bounded search scope; never turn a scoped non-match into a universal claim that no record exists.
