# Preventive Conservation Planning Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Object/material group, significance/use, location, and source.
- Risk pathway, exposure evidence, duration, and uncertainty.
- Current control and observed performance.
- Proposed preventive option, alternatives, and conflicting material needs.
- Target or trigger, units, evidence basis, and approval status.
- Owner, resources, review interval, and required monitoring.
- Treatment or specialist dependency and action status.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

A cabinet contains paper records and an unidentified metal object. Light-exposure records exist for the paper, but no environmental readings are supplied. The plan links display-duration review to the paper evidence, proposes establishing a cabinet baseline, and leaves a shared humidity target undecided. It does not transfer a paper-specific limit to the metal object.

## Failure Case

A plan orders rapid drying for every damp object without knowing materials or prior treatment. Replace the unsupported blanket action with documented condition priorities and a conservator handoff; retain non-invasive inventory and monitoring preparation where appropriate.
