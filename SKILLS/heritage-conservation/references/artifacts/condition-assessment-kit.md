# Conservation Condition Assessment Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Object/component ID, location, material claim, and identification source.
- Observation, date, observer or report, and source anchor.
- Image/measurement method, units, scale, and inspected extent.
- Observed feature versus inferred cause and confidence.
- Comparison baseline, comparability limit, and apparent change.
- Prior intervention, significance/use constraint, and unresolved dependency.
- Priority question, required examination, and responsible owner.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

Photo P1 shows a dark area on object O4; report R2 calls it corrosion, while the material is described only as metal. The review records the discoloration as visible, attributes the corrosion interpretation to R2, and leaves material and activity unresolved. A brighter later photo alone does not prove treatment success.

## Failure Case

A crack appears narrower in a later photograph taken from farther away. Reporting structural improvement without scale or matched capture conditions is unsupported. Record the images as non-comparable for width measurement and request an appropriate professional assessment.
