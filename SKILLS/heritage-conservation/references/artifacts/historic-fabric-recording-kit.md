# Historic Fabric Recording Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Place/component ID, parent, orientation, and source location.
- Drawing/photo/report reference, date, scale, units, and precision.
- Observed material, configuration, junction, and alteration evidence.
- Proposed phase/function, rationale, and competing interpretation.
- Condition reference and significance/use constraints.
- Inspected, obscured, inaccessible, absent, or unrecorded extent.
- Derived record version, correction, and restricted export field.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

Photo P4 shows a blocked opening in wall W2. Plan D1 locates W2 but has no scale. The register records the visible blocking, cites P4 and D1, and leaves its date and dimensions unresolved. A proposed later phase is retained as an interpretation from report R3, not as a measured or independently dated fact.

## Failure Case

A clean elevation drawing adds hidden wall thicknesses and dates all brickwork to one phase. Remove unsupported measurements from the derived record, preserve the source drawing, and mark the hidden fabric and phase assignment as unresolved.
