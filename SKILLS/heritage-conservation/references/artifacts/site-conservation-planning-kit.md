# Site Conservation Planning Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Site/component, location, significance value, and evidence source.
- Observed pressure, condition link, coverage, and competing cause.
- Conservation objective and conflict with use/access or other values.
- Option, expected effect, risk to fabric/deposits, and evidence gap.
- Priority rationale, dependencies, responsible owner, and decision status.
- Stage, proposed observation, review trigger, and completion evidence.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

A path channels visitors past an eroding bank beside a recorded wall. The plan links wear photographs to the affected segment, compares visitor-routing options, and identifies archaeological and accessibility questions before a route change. Drainage alteration stays a specialist-dependent option because no hydrological or below-ground evidence is supplied.

## Failure Case

A plan orders tree removal because roots might threaten masonry. Without evidence of cause, ownership, ecological constraints, or the effects of removal, this is an unsupported intervention. Record the observed condition and competing causes, then define the assessment and owner decision needed.
