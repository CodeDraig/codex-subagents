# Conservation Treatment Proposal Review Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Proposal version, object/component, purpose, and responsible professional.
- Condition/material/prior-treatment assertion and evidence anchor.
- Proposed action, expected benefit, loss/change risk, and significance constraint.
- Compatibility or other test evidence, its scope, and missing checks.
- Alternative including deferral where relevant and comparison criteria.
- Reversibility/retreatment claim and actual limitations.
- Baseline, documentation, follow-up criterion, and unresolved decision.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

A proposal would remove a coating from painted timber, but neither the coating nor the underlying paint has been identified. The review records the desired visibility improvement, the missing material and compatibility evidence, and possible loss of significant layers. It compares further examination and deferral as options without naming a solvent or declaring removal safe.

## Failure Case

A proposal calls a method fully reversible because an adhesive dissolves in a test tube. That does not establish removal from the actual substrate without residue or damage. Locate the unsupported inference and request evidence relevant to the object rather than endorsing the claim.
