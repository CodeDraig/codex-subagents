# Conservation Monitoring And Maintenance Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Object/component and observation ID, date, location, and source.
- Baseline reference, method, instrument, units, scale, and capture conditions.
- Observed value/feature, missing or suspect status, and comparability.
- Calculated change, time window, and limits of inference.
- Review trigger, source, applicable material/component, and status.
- Maintenance task, owner, proposed/due/completed state, and completion evidence.
- Follow-up observation, unresolved question, and handoff.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

Sensor S1 records 52% then 58% relative humidity for cabinet K1 under the same documented placement. A supplied cabinet-specific trigger calls for review above 55%, so the log flags review and reports a six-percentage-point increase. A wall crack photo has no matching scale and remains non-comparable. A scheduled inspection with no completion record stays due or unverified.

## Failure Case

A dashboard labels an object stable because this month has no readings. Preserve the missing interval, avoid a stability conclusion, and name the owner and baseline/observation needed. Do not substitute the last value as a new measurement.

A first crack-width reading of 0.6 mm is a baseline. With no earlier comparable reading, a trigger based on a 0.15 mm increase is not assessable; recording zero change or a negative trigger result would invent a comparison. An inspection with no completion evidence stays unverified, even when its due date has passed. A missing reading stays missing unless a separate record establishes that it was scheduled.
