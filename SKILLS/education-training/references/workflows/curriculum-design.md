# Curriculum Design

## Purpose

Designs learning outcomes, prerequisites, instructional sequences, and assessment alignment for courses or training programs before lesson authoring.

## Intake

Restate learner audience, prior knowledge, course purpose, contact and independent-study time, delivery format, source materials, and outcome owner. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Translate course aims into observable learner actions and distinguish required outcomes from optional enrichment.
2. Map prerequisites to outcomes and identify missing bridges before sequencing units; do not assume professional experience implies topic mastery.
3. Assign practice, feedback, and evidence of achievement to each outcome, then budget instruction, practice, assessment, and transitions against available time.
4. Build the course sequence with dependencies and alternative routes for known prior learning; explain omissions rather than quietly dropping outcomes.
5. Return an alignment map and feasibility questions, separating proposed curriculum from approved institutional requirements.

Read [curriculum-design-checklist.md](../artifacts/curriculum-design-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- An outcome requiring performance cannot be evidenced solely by recall questions.
- A prerequisite cycle requires a bridging unit or an owner decision, not arbitrary ordering.
- If the time budget cannot cover required practice, show the shortfall and scope alternatives without inventing additional contact hours.

## Validation

Trace every required outcome through a unit, practice opportunity, and assessment item or proposed method. Sum scheduled minutes and compare them with the supplied calendar; verify prerequisite direction and cite source sections for mandated content.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Learning Context`, `Outcome Map`, `Prerequisites`, `Sequence`, `Assessment Alignment`, `Feasibility`, `Owner Questions`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand lesson drafting to `lesson-materials-author`, scoring and item design to `assessment-designer`, access barriers to `learning-accessibility-reviewer`, and program-effectiveness questions to `training-evaluation-analyst`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before claiming accreditation, approving institutional curriculum, changing credential requirements, or making learner placement decisions. Missing outcome ownership permits a draft with decision questions, not invented approval.
