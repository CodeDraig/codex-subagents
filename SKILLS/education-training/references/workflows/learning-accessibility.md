# Learning Accessibility

## Purpose

Reviews learning materials and activities for participation barriers, usable alternatives, and verification needs without certifying accessibility or deciding individual accommodations.

## Intake

Restate learner task, material formats, delivery modality, stated access needs, available source files, intended platform, and review scope. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Trace the learner task from finding instructions through accessing content, producing work, receiving feedback, and recovering from errors.
2. Inspect headings, reading order, table structure, document language, links, contrast, keyboard paths where visible, captions, transcripts, and image descriptions.
3. Identify barriers caused by timing, bandwidth, sensory-only cues, complex language unrelated to the outcome, or required tools without alternatives.
4. Propose adaptations that preserve the learning objective and assessment construct; separate universal material improvements from individual accommodation decisions.
5. Record which formats and tasks were inspected, what requires assistive-technology testing, and what cannot be concluded from a screenshot or text export.

Read [learning-accessibility-checklist.md](../artifacts/learning-accessibility-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- A transcript may convey speech but omit a visually demonstrated procedure; identify necessary visual description.
- An adaptation should preserve the skill being assessed, not replace it with an unrelated easier task.
- Never infer disability or accommodation entitlement from learner performance or personal characteristics.

## Validation

Cite page, slide, timestamp, or task step for each barrier. Compare alternatives against the original outcome. Distinguish source inspection, keyboard or assistive-technology checks actually performed, and owner-only checks; do not certify conformance from automated or partial evidence.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Reviewed Learning Task`, `Barriers`, `Evidence`, `Adaptations`, `Assessment Equivalence`, `Verification Needed`, `Owner Questions`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand material edits to `lesson-materials-author`, rubric changes to `assessment-designer`, platform UI issues to `accessibility-reviewer`, and learner-data concerns to `privacy-compliance-reviewer`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before making medical judgments, deciding accommodations, modifying learner records, or certifying accessibility. Inaccessible formats leave those items unverified while review of available material continues.
