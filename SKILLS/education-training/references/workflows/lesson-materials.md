# Lesson Materials

## Purpose

Authors timed lesson plans, learner activities, worked examples, and facilitator notes from supplied learning outcomes and source material.

## Intake

Restate approved or draft outcomes, learner level, lesson duration, modality, group size, source packet, materials, and files to author. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Map the lesson to named outcomes and select a worked example that uses only supplied or verified subject content.
2. Sequence explanation, demonstration, guided practice, independent practice, and feedback as appropriate to the learner task.
3. Write learner instructions with inputs, expected work product, completion criteria, and a recovery route when an activity cannot be completed.
4. Add facilitator prompts, misconceptions, sample responses, timing cues, and material setup separately from learner-facing answers.
5. Check timing and source fidelity, then supply modality alternatives and unresolved content questions.

Read [lesson-materials-checklist.md](../artifacts/lesson-materials-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- Do not require paid accounts, specialist equipment, or inaccessible media unless the brief supplies access.
- A worked answer must explain its reasoning; keep instructor-only answer material separate when requested.
- If time is insufficient, propose explicit cuts or an additional session rather than silently shortening practice.

## Validation

Walk the timed plan against the lesson duration, complete each exercise from learner-visible inputs, verify answers against source locations, and check that every required attachment or link is present. Label activities not trialed with learners.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Lesson Context`, `Timed Plan`, `Learner Materials`, `Facilitator Notes`, `Checks For Understanding`, `Adaptations`, `Validation`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand outcome changes to `curriculum-designer`, formal scoring to `assessment-designer`, access barriers to `learning-accessibility-reviewer`, and disputed factual content to `source-verification-analyst`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before inventing subject facts, assigning grades, contacting learners, or publishing to a learning platform. Missing source content should produce a scoped outline and source request while separable lesson preparation continues.
