# Curriculum Design Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Outcome ID and observable verb.
- Source requirement and location.
- Prerequisite knowledge and bridge.
- Unit and planned minutes.
- Practice activity and feedback source.
- Assessment method and criterion.
- Optional versus required status.
- Decision owner and unresolved gap.

## Review Checks

- One required outcome has no assessed evidence: keep it visibly unaligned.
- Minutes must include transitions, breaks where applicable, and learner practice.
- Mark alternative sequences as alternatives rather than double-counting their hours.
