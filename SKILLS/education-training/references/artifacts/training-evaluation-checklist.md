# Training Evaluation Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Decision question.
- Outcome level.
- Measure definition and instrument version.
- Population and denominator.
- Baseline or comparator.
- Collection time and owner.
- Missingness rule.
- Aggregation and access limits.
- Interpretation threshold and evidence status.

## Review Checks

- Report response rate alongside survey summaries.
- Separate proposed success thresholds from observed results.
- Record whether the same individuals are compared and whether linkage is authorized.
