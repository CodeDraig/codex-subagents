# Collection Access Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Requested item and use.
- Restriction source, version, and clause.
- Applies-to scope.
- Embargo trigger and date.
- Separate rights or privacy constraint.
- Conflict and decision owner.
- Proposed surrogate or subset.
- Redaction verification need.
- Pending versus owner-approved status.

## Review Checks

- Do not apply a broad permission to excluded series.
- Approval evidence must identify both the item and intended use.
- Check hidden text and metadata when evaluating a supplied redacted derivative.
