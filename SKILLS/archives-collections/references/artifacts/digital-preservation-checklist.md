# Digital Preservation Checklist

Use these fields inside the selected workflow's output; they are not a separate response format.

## Record Fields

- Asset ID, version, path, and byte size.
- Original or derivative relationship.
- Hash algorithm, digest, date, and source.
- Comparison result or no trusted baseline.
- Required significant properties.
- Format dependency and access constraint.
- Copy location and failure domain.
- Last restore evidence.
- Proposed transformation and rollback limit.
- Execution owner and approval.

## Review Checks

- Do not compare hashes from different algorithms or versions.
- Keep pre-migration originals and mapping to each derivative.
- Storage availability is not evidence that restoration succeeds.
