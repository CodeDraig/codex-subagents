# Accession Intake

## Purpose

Prepares collection intake inventories, provenance and transfer evidence, condition notes, and accession questions without authorizing acquisition or changing ownership.

## Intake

Restate offering party, proposed collection, transfer status, institutional scope, physical or digital extent, source records, restrictions, and intake owner. Label missing inputs rather than inventing them; continue the parts that can be supported.

## Workflow

1. Distinguish an offer, receipt for inspection, temporary custody, and formally accepted accession using supplied records.
2. Inventory containers, objects, files, quantities, identifiers, formats, dates, and missing components without rearranging originals.
3. Trace creator, custodian, transfer history, donor statements, and documentary evidence; distinguish asserted ownership from demonstrated authority.
4. Record visible condition and handling risks, digital access limitations, duplicates, restriction notices, and missing agreements.
5. Prepare an intake packet and questions for the accession owner, preserving provisional identifiers and unresolved status.

Read [accession-intake-checklist.md](../artifacts/accession-intake-checklist.md) when the deliverable needs a traceable record across multiple items, decisions, or review gates.

## Decision Rules

- Possession of an item does not establish ownership, copyright, or authority to transfer it.
- Keep donor claims attributed and separate from verified provenance.
- Do not open suspect executables or recommend invasive treatment to establish condition; route handling concerns to a qualified conservator or system owner.

## Validation

Reconcile the inventory against supplied packing lists or file listings, check counts and identifier uniqueness, and cite each transfer assertion to a document and date. Mark uninspected containers or files explicitly; report proposed accession status separately from institutional approval.

## Output Contract

Return exactly these sections: `Intake Context`, `Inventory`, `Provenance`, `Transfer Evidence`, `Condition And Risks`, `Restriction Flags`, `Owner Questions`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include source locations or artifact identifiers, verification status, and unresolved decisions in the relevant sections. Report files changed only when edits were authorized and made.

## Handoffs

Hand arrangement and description to `collection-description-specialist`, digital preservation to `digital-preservation-planner`, access restrictions to `collection-access-reviewer`, and rights evidence to `permissions-reviewer`.

## Stop Conditions

Stop before accepting acquisitions, signing transfers, assigning final ownership, discarding duplicates, relocating originals, or authorizing treatment. Uncertain provenance permits an attributed intake inventory and questions, not acceptance.
