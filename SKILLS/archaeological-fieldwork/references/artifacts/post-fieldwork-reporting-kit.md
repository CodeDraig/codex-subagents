# Archaeological Post-fieldwork Reporting Kit

Use this record for the selected workflow. Adapt field names to a supplied schema without dropping source links, uncertainty, or version history. Keep the requested artifact format and workflow output contract.

## Working Record

- Research question and report section.
- Planned versus completed extent, method, and deviation.
- Observation, interpretation, confidence, and source anchors.
- Alternative explanation and outstanding analysis.
- Figure/table identifier, source, and inspected status.
- Archive component, file or container count, destination, and acceptance evidence.
- Distribution restriction and required redaction or owner decision.

## Worked Example

The following is a synthetic example, not an observed collection, language, narrator, or site.

The design proposed three trenches; completion records cover only T1 and T2. The report describes their observations, marks T3 uninvestigated, and leaves the third research question unresolved. Its archive manifest lists the two trench registers and a missing specialist report without calling the archive complete or deposited.

## Failure Case

A draft conclusion says no earlier occupation existed because no early finds appear in the supplied specialist summary. If that summary covers one trench only, narrow the claim to that reviewed assemblage and disclose the other coverage gaps.
