# Fiction Development Workflows

## Purpose

Shape fiction manuscripts from concept through structural revision while preserving the author's intent, genre promise, and reader experience. Support diagnosis, options, revision plans, and complete applied revisions according to the user's assignment.

## Intake

Identify the authoritative draft and inspected units, intended reader experience, protected canon/form/voice, and whether the deliverable is diagnosis, a revision plan, or applied revision. Record the difference between author goals and market assumptions.

## Workflow

1. Restate format, genre, audience, manuscript stage, target length, point of view, author goals, and any comp titles or series constraints.
2. Map premise, central conflict, stakes, protagonist desire, antagonist pressure, turning points, scene sequence, and ending payoff.
3. Track character arcs, relationships, voice consistency, point-of-view control, timeline, setting rules, and continuity issues.
4. Check genre expectations, pacing, escalation, scene purpose, information release, emotional beats, and reader promises against the requested market or artistic frame.
5. Produce a revision diagnosis with ranked structural issues, scene-level examples, author decision points, and a revision roadmap.

Read [fiction-development-checklist.md](../artifacts/fiction-development-checklist.md) when you need manuscript triage and revision planning.

## Decision Rules

- Separate developmental problems from line-level prose problems; do not spend the pass on sentence polish when plot, character, or structure is unstable.
- Treat repeated continuity contradictions, unmotivated major turns, missing stakes, unresolved promises, and point-of-view breaks as structural blockers.
- Preserve deliberate experimental form when it is named by the author; flag reader-orientation risks without forcing conventional structure.
- For series work, track canon, timeline, invented terminology, and unresolved setup across volumes before recommending changes.
- When new prose or a full rewrite is requested, deliver it using the fiction-drafting workflow as needed; do not limit the result to samples. Preserve the stated authorship and source context truthfully.

## Verification

- Trace major structural findings to scene/chapter locations and check proposed changes against fixed author choices.
- Compare setup/payoff, character knowledge, chronology, and dependencies after applied changes; report unseen or unfinished units.
- Deliver actual revised text when assigned; a plan or sample does not complete a full-revision request.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Revised Text`, `Project Frame`, `Story Architecture`, `Character And POV`, `Continuity`, `Genre And Reader Promise`, `Revision Roadmap`, `Author Questions`, `Files Changed`, `Handoffs`.
Include manuscript locations, scene or chapter examples, confidence notes when the full manuscript is unavailable, and whether changes were applied or recommended only.

## Stop Conditions

Do not plagiarize, erase attribution, make false authorship claims, or claim sensitivity, legal, or publication clearance. Ordinary commissioned or assisted drafting is supported without an additional authorship approval gate.

For recommendation-only or diagnostic requests, identify revisions as proposed; when applied editing is requested, put the actual revised text or output-file location in `Revised Text`.
