# Permissions Rights Review

## Purpose

Organize permissions and rights evidence for third-party content. Do not provide legal clearance opinions or final rights approval.

## Intake

Identify the actual reused item and source version, requested use, supplied permission/license or other reuse-basis evidence, format, territory, language, edition, term, and the decision the owner needs.

## Workflow

1. Restate work, content item, source, intended use, territory, format, edition, platform, and schedule.
2. Inventory ownership, rights holder, license, permission request, attribution, restrictions, fees, exclusivity, term, and evidence path.
3. Match the requested use to license scope and publication requirements, including transformations, excerpts, translation, marketing, cover use, and digital distribution.
4. Flag missing permissions, unclear ownership, incompatible license terms, fair-use review boundaries, and legal-review needs.
5. Produce a rights tracker, blocker list, and owner questions.

Read [permissions-rights-checklist.md](../artifacts/permissions-rights-checklist.md) when you need clearance tracking.

## Decision Rules

- Require an evidence path and identified scope before treating a permission as documented. Record other claimed reuse bases separately with their evidence and unresolved interpretation; do not manufacture a grant.
- A license must cover the actual medium, territory, term, edition, and distribution channel; partial coverage is a blocker until resolved.
- Compare transformations, excerpt length, marketing/cover use, language, and derivative formats with the documented reuse basis. Flag gaps for the owner rather than assuming universal coverage or prescribing a legal conclusion.
- Fair-use analysis is a review boundary only; if final legal clearance is needed, escalate rather than guessing.

## Verification

- Trace every reported permission/restriction to a supplied document and the specific item/use it covers.
- Compare formats, language, territory, term, attribution, and transformation conditions without assuming missing terms.
- Separate documented permission status, unsupported reuse assertions, and issues requiring qualified legal judgment; do not treat inventory completion as clearance.

## Output Contract

Return exactly: `Rights Scope`, `Content Inventory`, `Permission Status`, `License Restrictions`, `Attribution Needs`, `Risks`, `Owner Questions`, `Handoffs`.
Include the evidence path and the exact reuse boundaries that were checked.

## Stop Conditions

Do not provide legal clearance, disregard documented restrictions, remove required attribution, or fabricate rights evidence. Missing or uncertain reuse evidence requires a located query; continue the inventory and unaffected preparation without representing that use as cleared.
