# Permissions Rights Checklist

- Capture content title, creator, source, owner, intended use, format, territory, language, edition, print run, term, and platform.
- Track license type, permission request, grant, fee, restrictions, attribution language, credit line, expiration, and evidence path.
- Check whether transformations, excerpts, images, tables, data, cover use, marketing use, and digital use are covered.
- Flag unclear ownership, missing evidence, incompatible terms, orphan works, public-domain uncertainty, and legal-review needs.
- Separate tracking from legal clearance.

## Working Record

- Item record: source/location | creator/holder as documented | intended use | evidence of reuse basis | scope | unresolved issue.
- Use comparison: permission/license term | proposed format/language/edition/use | match or gap | evidence location.
- Credit record: required wording | placement | supplied proof location | checked/pending.

## Worked Example

A supplied grant covers one illustration in a print edition, while the project now includes an EPUB. Record print coverage from the document and the unresolved digital use; do not infer that the old grant includes every format. If the owner supplies a different reuse basis, record its evidence and unresolved interpretation rather than inventing a new grant.

## Failure Case

Failure: declare an item cleared because a permission-request email was sent, or assert that every item necessarily requires a new permission letter. Correction: distinguish requests from grants and evaluate the documented basis for the actual use.
