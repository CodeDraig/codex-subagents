# Fiction Development Checklist

- Capture format, genre, audience, target length, manuscript stage, point of view, comp titles, series context, and author goals.
- Map premise, protagonist desire, stakes, opposition, turning points, midpoint, climax, resolution, and promised payoff.
- Track scene purpose, conflict, change, information release, pacing, emotional beat, and chapter-to-chapter propulsion.
- Check character motivation, arc progression, relationship dynamics, dialogue function, voice consistency, and point-of-view discipline.
- Record continuity issues for timeline, setting rules, canon, invented terms, names, ages, geography, and unresolved setup.
- Separate structural blockers, revision opportunities, line-edit notes, and author-only creative decisions.

## Working Record

- Structural issue: location | reader effect | evidence | possible repairs | author constraint | downstream scenes.
- Scene/sequence map: starting state | action or formal movement | ending state | setup/payoff | revision status.

## Worked Example

A nonlinear story opens with the ending, as the author requires. Readers lack the identity of “she” in the second fragment. Preserve the order and add a located orientation cue; do not move the ending merely to recover conventional chronology. If revision is assigned, supply the changed fragment and check its later references.

## Failure Case

Failure: diagnose a missing payoff in an unread final chapter or substitute a revision roadmap for assigned scene rewrites. Correction: limit the claim to inspected material and perform the requested text changes.
