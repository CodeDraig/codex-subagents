# Publication Proofreading: Working Kit

## Working Record

- Correction row: proof/version | locator | current text | replacement | basis | applied/rechecked status.
- Proof pass: text comparison; headings/notes; captions/tables; page furniture; prior corrections.
- Reflow dependency: changed passage | pagination affected? | index/cross-reference check | next proof.

## Worked Example

Copy says “public access”; proof says “pubic access”. Record the proof page and exact replacement, verify the copy reading, and recheck the corrected line. A previously requested note-number correction remains open if the new proof still has the old number.

## Failure Case

Failure: declare the PDF proof clean from extracted text alone or rewrite a whole paragraph for taste. Correction: report text-only coverage and route substantive rewriting to the relevant editor.
