# Production Workflow Checklist

- Capture publication type, series or journal, house style, manuscript status, audience, deadlines, and owner roles.
- Track front matter, chapters, back matter, figures, tables, references, captions, alt text, permissions, proofs, and index.
- Record style-sheet decisions for spelling, capitalization, numbering, citations, abbreviations, terminology, and exceptions.
- Flag missing assets, inconsistent references, unresolved queries, production blockers, and rights issues.
- Keep coordination distinct from final editorial, legal, or publisher approval.

## Working Record

- Stage record: input version | deliverable | dependency | owner | proposed/complete | evidence | open queries.
- Asset ledger: component | filename/version | source | completeness | correction status | next check.
- Late-change map: changed unit | proof/reflow | notes/contents/index | metadata/export | recheck status.

## Worked Example

A late paragraph insertion shifts a chapter by one page. Mark the text correction complete only where applied; reopen affected contents/index locators and the PDF proof check. The EPUB may need regeneration but does not inherit a print-page correction automatically.

## Failure Case

Failure: mark every stage complete because a schedule lists dates or an export command exits successfully. Correction: attach actual artifact/check evidence to each completed stage.
