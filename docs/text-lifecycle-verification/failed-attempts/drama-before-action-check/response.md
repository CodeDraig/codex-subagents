# Dramatic Text

## THE KEY

*A bare room. One table stands at center. MINA is at its near edge. SOL is opposite her. Between them: a brass key.*

MINA: Give it back.

SOL: It is back. Look at it.

MINA: On the table is not back.

SOL: Then reach.

*MINA extends a hand. SOL lays two fingers over the key, not touching her.*

MINA: Sol.

SOL: You have said my name like a door shutting all week.

MINA: I said give it back.

SOL: Ask.

MINA: I am asking.

SOL: For the metal. Not for me.

*MINA withdraws her hand. The key remains under SOL's fingers.*

MINA: You went into my coat.

SOL: Yes.

MINA: You knew I would find it gone.

SOL: Yes.

MINA: That was the point?

SOL: No. That was the part you would notice.

*A silence. MINA turns the table a fraction, then stops; it does not move.*

MINA: Why did you take it?

SOL: Because you carry it when you do not intend to leave. Because every time we disagree, you put your hand in that pocket. I wanted to know whether you would stay without it.

MINA: You could have asked.

SOL: I am asking now.

MINA: No. You are making me answer your question with my house locked behind it.

*SOL lifts his fingers. He pushes the key toward MINA. It stops halfway.*

SOL: I was afraid you would go.

MINA: I was afraid you wanted me to prove I would not.

*MINA takes the key, considers her pocket, then sets it upright on the table between them.*

MINA: It stays here tonight.

SOL: Between us?

MINA: Where neither of us can steal it.

*SOL places one open hand beside the key. After a beat, MINA places hers on the other side. The key stands between their hands.*

# Character And Action Notes

Mina pursues return, then refuses to let possession resolve the breach. Sol withholds fear of abandonment until Mina asks why.

# Staging And Continuity
