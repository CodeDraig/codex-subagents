# Dramatic Text

## THE KEY

*One room. One table at center. MINA stands at the near end; SOL stands at the far end. SOL's closed fist rests on the tabletop.*

MINA: Return it.

SOL: Ask me.

MINA: I have been asking since the door.

SOL: You have been naming an object.

*MINA leans across the table. SOL lifts the fist just clear of her reach.*

MINA: You went through my coat.

SOL: Yes.

MINA: You took my key.

SOL: Yes.

MINA: Give it back.

SOL: Why did I take it?

MINA: That is not a question you get to set after stealing from me.

SOL: It is the only question you have not asked.

*MINA puts both palms flat on the table. SOL lowers the fist but does not open it.*

MINA: Fine. Why?

SOL: Because whenever we stop talking, you touch that pocket. Once. Then twice. As if the key is already deciding for you.

MINA: It opens my apartment.

SOL: I know.

MINA: It is not a threat.

SOL: I thought you might say that if you had to say anything.

*A silence. MINA looks at SOL's fist, then at SOL.*

MINA: I would have come back for it.

SOL: I know.

MINA: Not for you. For the key.

*SOL opens the fist. A small key lies in the center of the palm. SOL sets it on the table and draws the hand away.*

SOL: There. It is yours.

*MINA takes the key. She closes her fingers around it, then opens them and returns it to the exact center of the table.*

MINA: Tonight it belongs to neither of us.

SOL: What does that mean?

MINA: It means I leave because I choose to. Or I stay for the same reason.

*SOL nods. Neither reaches for the key.*
