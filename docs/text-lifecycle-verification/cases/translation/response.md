## Translation

“They did not promise to return; they said they might write. The letter arrived two days later.”

## Translation Decisions

Singular “they” preserves the subject’s unspecified gender. “Might write” retains the uncertainty and limited commitment of “quizá escribiría.”

## Terminology Notes

“Volver” is rendered as “return”; “carta” as “letter.”

## Coverage And Checks

Complete translation of both sentences. Negation, uncertainty, sequence, and temporal detail are preserved without adding gender or certainty.
