## Dramatic Text

*A bare room. One table. MINA stands at its near edge. SOL sits at the far edge, one hand flat over a key.*

**MINA:** Give it back.

**SOL:** Ask for it.

**MINA:** I just did.

**SOL:** You issued a receipt.

*Mina reaches. Sol closes his hand. The key does not move.*

**MINA:** It opens my door.

**SOL:** Yes.

**MINA:** Then stop making it a lesson.

**SOL:** Ask me why I took it.

**MINA:** You took it because you like having the last thing in your hand.

**SOL:** That is not a question.

*Mina circles the table, then stops beside Sol. She could touch his shoulder; she does not.*

**MINA:** Why did you take it?

*Sol looks at his closed fist.*

**SOL:** Because every time you leave, you say you’ll come back. I wanted one thing that could make you say where.

**MINA:** A key can’t do that.

**SOL:** No. But it made you stay three minutes.

*Mina sits on the table’s near corner. Sol opens his hand but keeps the key in his palm.*

**MINA:** I was going to my sister’s.

**SOL:** You never said.

**MINA:** You never asked.

**SOL:** I’m asking badly now.

*A silence. Mina turns Sol’s open palm upward with one finger. The key glints between them.*

**MINA:** Next time, ask before you steal.

**SOL:** Next time, answer before you disappear.

*Mina takes the key. She studies it, then sets it at the center of the table instead of pocketing it.*

**MINA:** It stays here tonight.

**SOL:** So do you?

**MINA:** Until you ask me something better.

*Sol leaves the key untouched. Mina does too.*

## Character And Action Notes

Mina’s objective is the key’s return; Sol’s is to make Mina ask why it was taken. Mina shifts from demand to inquiry; Sol shifts from control to an imperfect request.

## Staging And Continuity

Only Mina and Sol are present. The single table remains center stage. The key begins under Sol’s hand, moves to Sol’s open palm, then to the table’s center. Mina’s first reach fails; she later takes the key before choosing to leave it accessible to both.
