## Manuscript Draft

This exploratory observational study examined whether self-reported daily reading time changed over two weeks among adult volunteers. Twenty-four adults provided self-reports of reading time during the observation period. Mean reported reading increased by 3 minutes per day across the two weeks. This descriptive change addresses the study question at the level of the participating volunteers and indicates that reported reading time was higher over the observed period. However, the design does not establish that the observed increase was caused by any intervention or by the passage of time. The absence of a control group prevents comparison with concurrent patterns that might otherwise account for the change. In addition, the result is based on self-reported reading time, which may be affected by reporting error or changes in how participants recorded their reading. No uncertainty interval or significance test was supplied; accordingly, the precision of the estimated 3-minute increase and its compatibility with chance variation cannot be assessed from the available information. Findings should therefore be interpreted as exploratory and limited to the reported two-week observations in this volunteer sample.
