## Revised Text

We waited for the bell. We waited for the door. We waited for the name to be called. The clerk said the delay might be temporary—might.

## Rhetorical Decisions

The repeated waits retain their measured escalation; the final isolated “might” leaves the assurance unfinished.

## Meaning And Voice Checks

All three “We waited” instances are preserved. The clerk’s statement remains explicitly uncertain.
