## Located Observations

- Opening sentence: “snapshot,” “manifest,” and “generation” are undefined at first use.
- “after exporting” requires readers to know what exporting the manifest entails and whether completion can be verified.
- “the retained generation” has no established referent.
- “first” leaves the operational sequence unclear: reconcile before deletion, before export, or before another unstated step.

## Predicted Reader Effects

- A first-time user may pause to infer the objects, their relationships, and the safe order of actions.
- The deletion command may create avoidable hesitation because its prerequisite and recovery implications are not oriented.
- This appears to be accidental procedural obstruction, not intended productive difficulty: the supplied form is a first-time-user guide.

## Revision Options

- At the first occurrence, orient each specialized term or link it to a prior definition; preserve the technical vocabulary if it is required by the system.
- State the dependency that makes deletion safe, including what “exporting” confirms.
- Establish which generation is retained and why reconciliation precedes the next action.
- Make the intended order explicit without changing the guide’s concise imperative form.
