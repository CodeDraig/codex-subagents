Poem

Still, rain beads on the yellow safety line.  
Still, a pigeon worries a torn ticket by the bin.  
Still, the rails hold two black mirrors of the sky.  
Still, the departure board blinks its single red wound.  
Still, wind lifts newspaper against the locked kiosk.  
Still, a cold bench keeps the shape of no one.  
Still, beneath the clock, I count the swallowed minutes.  
Still, I wait here not for a train, but for the platform to remember me.
